Camera response requires a paid native gesture and public terrain projection proving that the original alert marker becomes visible. The group camera gesture also requires the actual first group recall, native second key within six ticks, actual living own group centroid, selection and applied jump. A group recall alone has no camera endpoint proof.

The additional command endpoint is a native accepted move or attack-move bringing armed own defenders toward the public danger locus. Every actor must appear in the own public roster and minimap within 48 m of the marker both at creation and queue time. Every actual formation destination must be within 24 m of the marker and approach it by at least 2.5 m. These are prospective purpose rules: 48/24 m use the game's existing observed-event locality rules in `shared/ai-priority.js`, and 2.5 m uses the arrival tolerance already documented by the screen response adapter. They do not alter any timing threshold or claim that the tactical decision was optimal. Exact alert recipient IDs are never inferred from text.

An earlier selection in this exact accepted operation may be its first action only if its native result changes selection, selects exactly the intended own actors, and the later accepted order preserves the entire original actor set. The native submission result, command descriptor, actual selection and completed fire input must agree. Preparing a future gesture, a partially selected formation, an earlier queued operation, a refused order, or an operation serving another area supplies no endpoint proof. A causal label alone is insufficient.

Creation and queue snapshots contain own roster identities and positions, anonymous minimap coordinates and sides, and actual selected IDs. They contain no foe IDs, foe health, foe exact types, authoritative game object or raw detector graph. Public terrain cells are captured only for camera projection. These records remain outside commander memory. A group-binding key that changes neither selection nor camera and submits no order is known irrelevant to the endpoint. Unsupported production, construction, abilities, retreats and ambiguous selections remain unknown. A missing accepted purpose does not silently exclude an input.
