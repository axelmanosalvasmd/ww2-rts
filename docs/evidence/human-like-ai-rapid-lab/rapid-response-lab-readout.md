Sampling the delivered public frame over successive frame intervals attributes about 34 to 36 seconds to active physical motor work or paid reaction, about 15 to 16 seconds to empty retry pauses, about 3.4 seconds to deliberation and the remainder to queued or otherwise empty hands. Empty pauses alone do not prove useful work was ignored: squads already fighting, digging or holding remain protected. Repeated rejected cover orders are a concrete avoidable waste. Shortening all dwell or adding inputs would not address it.

The original planner considers ordinary Hard retreat only below 50% health. Its attended protective destination also requires that health condition or suppressed damage. This scene has normal suppression, a stationary squad and 65% health. The nearest fully escaped cell also lies outside the existing local search: the medium is 14m away with range 38m, so a 4m safety margin needs at least 28m of travel, exceeding the existing 24m bound. The separate proposed fallback uses this public geometry and actual heavy-hit cue while preserving the ordinary health thresholds.

The authored 100 to 35 HP scenes expose the strict Easy and Normal retreat threshold edge and should not be used to justify tuning it. All nine late medium contacts per difficulty are also censored. Those controls intentionally retain held-fire rifles and cooling grenades against armor. They demonstrate an adverse tactic case, not a universal infantry baseline or a reason to broaden monitoring exemptions.
