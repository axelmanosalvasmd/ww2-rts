Controls

Mouse

Left click
Select a unit or building
Drag
Select units in a box
Shift + click / drag
Add to or remove from your selection
Double-click
Select nearby units of the same type; Ctrl/Command includes the whole map
Right-click
Move, attack, garrison, or interact with the target
Shift + right-click
Queue the order
Ctrl + right-click
Attack-move, fighting anything met along the way
Right-click-drag
Set a destination and facing for the formation; release to confirm
Wheel
Zoom at the cursor
Middle-drag
Rotate the camera
Map edge
Move the cursor to an edge to scroll
Minimap
Left-drag to move the camera; right-click to order
Alt + left-click
Ping a spot for your team
While aiming
Click to place or call in; move to aim direction. Right-click cancels

Camera

WASD / arrows
Pan
Q / E
Rotate left / right
F1
Open or close this sheet
F2
Show or hide the stats overlay (pick the numbers in the menu)
M
Mute or unmute audio

Units and orders

X / R
Stop / retreat selected units
F / G
Use a ready ability / aim attack-move
Shift+E
Unload the selected halftrack
Shift+C
Take cover
Shift+F, Shift+G, Shift+X
Toggle hold fire, hold position, auto-retreat
Ctrl+A
Select your whole army
. / Shift+.
Select next idle unit / all idle units
,
Select the next idle Engineer
Space / Shift+Space
Jump to newest alert or selection / follow selection
1–9
Recall a control group; tap twice to center
Ctrl+1–Ctrl+9
Set a control group; Shift adds to it
Alt+1–Alt+9
Move selected units to a control group and remove them from other groups
Alt+H
Open or close Alert history
Alt+Left / Alt+Right
Visit the previous or next Alert at its recorded location
H / Shift+H
Go home in Classic / set army rally point
Esc
Clear selection, cancel aiming, or release captured mouse
